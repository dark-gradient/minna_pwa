Basic Kanji 320 — Stroke Order, Meanings and Example Sentences
All 320 kanji from the MLC (Meguro Language Center) book "Basic Kanji 320" (JLPT N5/N4), in the book's own order.
For each kanji: the number-and-stroke-order diagram as printed in the book (the small numbers show the order and direction of the strokes), the Japanese (kun) and Chinese (on) readings, the kanji's own meaning where the book gives one, and the book's vocabulary with meanings and example sentences. A word with several meanings has one row per meaning.
Notes: Example sentences are exactly as printed in the book, which gives them in Japanese only (no English translation). The small furigana above some kanji and the book's ★/◆ importance marks are left out.
